# MVP SPRINT 3 – Maritime Logistics Analysis and Dashboard Optimization

## Objective of MVP


* What problem does it solve?

It solves the lack of visibility and understanding of Brazil's maritime transportation flow, allowing users to analyze the volume of imported and exported products, identify the main causes of delays at ports, and understand how cargo distribution can be optimized to avoid logistical bottlenecks.

* What hypothesis will be validated?

The hypothesis to be validated is that better visualization and analysis of maritime logistics data can help identify port congestion causes, improve decision-making, and support the selection of the most suitable ports for each region, reducing delays and increasing operational efficiency.

* What value will be delivered to the end user?

The end user will receive a clear and interactive view of Brazil's maritime logistics scenario, enabling informed decisions regarding import and export operations, reducing logistical risks, minimizing delays, and improving supply chain planning.
