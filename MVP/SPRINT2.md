# SPRINT2 – Cargo Flow and Port Efficiency Analysis

## Objective of MVP


* What problem does it solve?

The proposed features address the lack of centralized and accessible information regarding port terminal efficiency and halted cargo operations. By consolidating operational indicators and interruption data into an interactive dashboard, decision-makers can more easily identify bottlenecks, operational inefficiencies, and factors impacting cargo flow across ports.

* What hypothesis will be validated?

The project aims to validate the hypothesis that interactive visualizations and operational analytics can improve the understanding of port performance and support more effective public policy decision-making. It is also expected to validate whether analyzing halted cargo operations helps identify recurring operational issues and opportunities for infrastructure or process improvements.

* What value will be delivered to the end user?

The end user will gain access to strategic and data-driven insights about port operations, terminal efficiency, and cargo interruptions. The dashboard will support faster analysis, improved monitoring of operational performance, and more informed decision-making for planning, logistics, and public policy development.
